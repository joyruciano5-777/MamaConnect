# Tests

## Test 1 - CHW records an emergency assessment and it reaches the facility desk [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: authentication, role onboarding, patient creation, automatic referral trigger, realtime sync from CHW to Facility view, database persistence after reload
Description: Verifies a CHW can sign in, record a high-risk assessment, and see it show up on the assigned facility's referral desk without a manual refresh.

Steps:
1. Sign in
2. Choose the "Community Health Worker" role, select a facility, and continue
3. Open "New assessment", fill in Name "Test Mother", Age 28, Gestational weeks 32, and check "Vaginal bleeding"
4. Save the assessment
5. Refresh the page

Expected: The patient "Test Mother" appears in the CHW list tagged "REFER NOW — EMERGENCY" and a referral status is visible, and both remain visible after the refresh.

## Test 2 - Facility staff acknowledges a referral and marks arrival
Viewport: desktop
Covers: role-scoped facility view, referral acknowledge/arrive workflow, database update, persistence after reload
Description: Verifies a facility-role user can acknowledge an incoming referral and mark it arrived, and the state persists.

Setup:
1. As a CHW, record an assessment with "Convulsions" checked to generate an emergency referral to a facility

Steps:
1. Sign in as facility staff for the facility the referral was sent to (selecting that role/facility during onboarding)
2. Find the incoming referral and click "Acknowledge"
3. Click "Mark arrived"
4. Refresh the page

Expected: The referral shows status "Arrived" after the refresh.

## Test 3 - Facility staff adjusts commodity stock
Viewport: mobile
Covers: commodity ledger update, database persistence after reload, mobile layout
Description: Verifies stock quantity changes save and survive a reload.

Steps:
1. Sign in as facility staff
2. On the commodity ledger, note the current Oxytocin quantity and tap "+" three times
3. Refresh the page

Expected: The Oxytocin quantity is 3 higher than the original value after the refresh.

## Test 4 - District oversight reroutes a referral
Viewport: desktop
Covers: district-role access boundary, reroute workflow, cross-view aggregation
Description: Verifies district oversight can reroute a referral and the "Rerouted after dispatch" stat updates.

Setup:
1. As a CHW, record an assessment with high blood pressure (systolic 170, diastolic 115) to generate an emergency referral

Steps:
1. Sign in as district oversight
2. Open the District tab and find the pending referral
3. Click "Reassign facility" and send it to an alternate facility

Expected: The "Rerouted after dispatch" stat count increases by 1 and the referral's facility updates.

## Test 5 - CHW role cannot access the Facility or District tabs
Viewport: desktop
Covers: role-based access boundary, negative permission test
Description: Verifies a CHW-role account only sees the CHW workspace, not facility or district tools.

Steps:
1. Sign in and choose the "Community Health Worker" role during onboarding

Expected: Only the CHW tab is visible in the navigation; no Facility, Map, or District tab is shown.
